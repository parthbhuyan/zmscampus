About Zion Mission School

Shaping Holistic Excellence in Education

Located in Amba and operated by the Zion Mission Society in Nirjuli, our school believes in the transformative power of education beyond traditional classrooms.

Our Mission

Imparting students the ‘TRUTH’ and positive virtues like courage, vitality, confidence, and creativity to nurture visionary leaders of upright character.

Aims & Objectives

Providing quality education at an affordable cost using modern progressive techniques for all-round physical, intellectual, and moral development.

2-Acre Green Campus

Sprawled across 2 acres of lush green grounds in Amba, Doimukh, featuring airy classrooms, modern furniture, and a tranquil learning ambiance.
