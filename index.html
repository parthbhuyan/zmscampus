About Zion Mission School

Shaping Holistic Excellence in Education

Located in Amba and operated by the Zion Mission Society, Nirjuli, the school looks beyond the classroom — building character, curiosity and academic distinction together.

Our Mission

To help every student live by the truth and grow in courage, confidence and creativity — nurturing visionary leaders of upright character.

Aims & Objectives

Quality education at an affordable cost, using modern, progressive teaching methods for all‑round physical, intellectual and moral development.

2‑Acre Green Campus

Spread across two acres in Amba, Doimukh, roughly 3 km from Nirjuli town — airy classrooms, modern furniture and a calm learning setting.

Zion Mission School is proud of the commitment its staff bring to an all‑round education for every student. Together, we celebrate academic, cultural and sporting achievements as one community, moving forward with confidence and pride under the school's guiding proverb: “Time is precious.”

MA Mahaishang Angkang Principal, Zion Mission School Official website
ZMS Campus ERP Platform

Integrated Management & Academic Intelligence

Built specifically for ZMS faculty and administrators, for real‑time academic workflows from first period to final report card.

Period‑Wise Attendance

Class 9–12 daily registers with automatic low‑attendance warnings below the 75% CBSE threshold and per‑student SID tracking.

CBSE Syllabus Tracker

Unit‑by‑unit completion for IT, Science, Humanities and Commerce, with automated progress percentages per class.

One‑Click PDF & XLS Reports

Generate official class registers, exam gradebooks and summary sheets ready to print or share, in a single click.

Security & Audit Logs

Every edit is timestamped and traceable, with a strict 24‑hour lock window to protect finalised records.

Academic Calendar

Pre‑loaded school events, exam windows, national holidays and celebration reminders for the whole year.

Progressive Web App

Installable on phones and desktops, with offline support and a home‑screen launcher icon — no app store needed.
